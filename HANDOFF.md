# Budget Tracker: handoff for a new session

Read this first. It describes how the app is built, where everything lives, the owner's preferences, and how to work safely.

## What it is
A household budget PWA for **Arhaanbeta** (aarhaan22@gmail.com). One file app: `index.html` (+ `sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `icons/`), hosted on **GitHub Pages**: https://arhaanbeta.github.io/budget-tracker/. Data lives in a private Google Sheet, **"Budget Tracker data"** (owner aarhaan22@gmail.com), reached through a **Google Apps Script web app** (`apps-script/Code.gs`).

## App structure (index.html)
- **Sync:** optimistic outbox (`queue` → `flush`), full data returned by the script each time; 30 s polling; offline-safe. Ops: config, txn, deltxn, bal, delbal, fix, replace, adduser, deluser, vput, vdel, signout.
- **Overview:** (phones in a Chrome tab: a dismissible "Install the app" card), then the hero with Spent as the headline (bar against the month's budget + even-pace marker) and Income, Saved, Savings rate beside it, then one tile per category in Setup order (Cash in hand tile first if set), each with its category icon. No group labels or commentary (owner's explicit wish). Below the tiles: **Credit cards** (`cardStripHtml`: the three KPIs + split bar, then a `.wgrid` of gradient `.wc` cards in bank colours, most urgent first: overdue (red glow, pulsing pill) → due within 5 days (amber pill) → other unpaid → next bill → all clear; each shows To pay / Next bill / Owed, Pay By, last 4 and network from the Vault when unlocked, and a limit bar) and **Bank accounts** (`bankStripHtml`: the Add entry Bank buttons in `config.payBanks` order, else every non-stand-in Bank account; live balance via `X.res`, total in the header). `wcSplit` puts the bank on top and the person/card name under it; `wcThemes` turns repeats of the same bank round the colour wheel (`wcHue`) so the three HDFC accounts differ. Cards rise in staggered and get one light sweep while the view has `.enter`; amounts count up (`wcCount`, from `viewEntered` and `renderOverview`); all motion is off under reduced motion. Tapping a card opens its breakdown (`card:` / `w-acct:`). The old dated bill rows (`.ccr`) are gone.
- **Categories:** owner's canonical names; `misc` = "Miscellaneous", `disc` = "Discretionary" (`unplanned: true` → **no budget (0)**; its spending counts in Spent and shows purple, but adds nothing to the budget — owner's rule), `schtr` = "School transport". Quick-pick buttons: `config.chips` = [groc, misc, schtr, car] pinned first; short labels `short` (groc → "Household", misc → "Misc"), now a visible **Button label** column in Setup; blank uses the full name. One name per category everywhere (tiles, entries, filters, breakdowns). Do NOT reintroduce Fixed/Variable/Sinking/Discretionary group labels anywhere visible.
- **Entry form:** Amount (calculator: + − × ÷, brackets, dictation words; `parseAmount` is a safe parser, never eval) with + − × ÷ = buttons → Category buttons (+ dictation box) → **Paid with: Cash (default) / Bank / Card** → Date: Today / Yesterday / 📅 → Note → collapsed "More" (for whom, tag, refund). The five text boxes are `contenteditable` divs (`makeTextBox`) so Chrome/SwiftKey autofill bars don't appear; they expose `.value`.
- **Payment data:** cash → `acct='cash'`; bank → `acct=<account id>`; card → `card=<card id>`; card bill payment → both (card being paid + bank). Old entries without payment stay unset unless edited.
- **Vault (Owner only, open mode):** no passphrase; rows AES-GCM with a built-in key (`vOpenKey`, SHA-256 of `budget-tracker/open-vault/v1`), meta `{v:2, mode:'open'}`. Local copy in localStorage for instant open; changes queue and sync in the background. Tables: Credit cards (CC, Name, CC number, Exp, CVV, Stmt, PW, Cust ID, Note), Savings accounts (Account, Name, Acc, Cust ID, PW, IFSC, Card no, Exp, CVV, Notes), Personal (Portal/item, ID/number, Password/note). Filters: All, Credit cards, Savings accounts, Personal, Aadhaar, Voter. Paste many rows (heading-matched), Delete all (type DELETE), per-cell copy.
- **Vault look (owner-approved):** no tables. Hero summary (counts, tap to filter); credit cards drawn as real cards (`vCardFace`: bank colours via `vTheme`, chip, network from the number via `vNet`, last 4 only on the face, name, expiry, bill day); bank accounts drawn exactly like the credit cards (`vBankFace`, class `vcc vacc`: bank colours, bank emblem instead of the chip, debit-card network or SAVINGS, A/C last 4 only, holder, IFSC and Cust ID), personal items as a list (`vPersonalRow`). Tap a card or account and its details appear ON the card itself (`vOnCard`: every filled field with label, selectable value, copy button; values exactly as saved, numbers only spaced for reading and copied as digits only; codes over 10 characters get a full-width box so they never break). Tapping a value never closes the card; tapping the card again closes it. Personal items still open `vDetails` below the row. Section actions: + Add, Paste many, ⋯ (Rename columns, Delete all). Nothing scrolls sideways.
- **Vault → payment lists:** `syncPayMethods()` copies **names only** (+ last 4 digits when names clash) and statement days into Setup data: `config.cards` (vault card ids, `src:'vault'`, `dueDay` kept by id, else due ≈ statement + 20 days) and `config.payBanks` (account ids in order: HDFC ARHAAN, AMMI HDFC, HDFC EFRAH, then the rest). Vault-only banks become `config.accounts` entries with `pay:true` (hidden from Wealth/Setup editor). Family phones get the buttons without vault access.
- **Wealth (owner's rule):** liabilities are paid from liquid money, so **Liquid money = liquid assets − all liabilities (loans, money owed, credit cards) − this month's budget still to spend**, and **Net worth = liquid money + non-liquid assets** (`wealthCalc`). Headline tiles: Net worth, Liquid money, Liabilities, **Gross assets** (liquid + non-liquid, before liabilities; owner removed Months of cover). Order: tiles, Assets, Debts, Still to spend, Not counted, then chart, Record today's balances, snapshots. **FD growth** view: FD accounts at latest balance, monthly compounding at 7% (editable, stored in prefs).
- **Setup:** cycle day (25), categories/budgets (with Button label), accounts (net worth), **Credit cards**, **Adjustments**, account & sync, install-as-app, family access, backup. No cash box.
- **Live balances (owner's rule):** bank and cash accounts (kind Bank/Cash, not `pay`) = last recorded balance + every entry tagged to them dated after that record (income/refunds +, spends −, card-bill payments and other transfers −; ATM cash from a bank is − there and + to cash in hand). Worked out in `ctx()`, so `X.latest[id]` holds `{v (live), date, rec (recorded), moves, delta}` and every screen uses it. Re-tie by recording a balance. Every account's breakdown has **Update balance** (`set-one` → `openBalances(id)`): a one-account form (e.g. EPF, NPS monthly), pre-filled. `saveBalances()` MERGES with figures already recorded that date (the script's putBalance replaces the whole date, so never send a partial date unmerged). Other kinds (FD, loans…) keep the recorded figure.
- **Balances engine (owner's rule: every entry moves its balance; made infallible Nov 2026):** one function, `moneyMoves(t, X)`, says what an entry does to bank/cash (Income +; Expense −, refund +; card-bill payment − from the bank; ATM − bank + cash in hand; other Transfers −, or + when the refund box, labelled "Money coming in", is ticked). Card side stays in `cardLedger`. Accounts resolve through `acctResolver` (`X.res`, `t._acct`): `'cash'` → the Cash account whatever its id; a Vault stand-in (`pay`) → the Wealth account linked to the same Vault row (`a.vault`) or `into`. **Record time:** balances typed for TODAY also save `time:<acctId>` = ms in the same Balances date (rows in the sheet, no script change); `afterRecord()` counts an entry if dated later, or same day with `Created at` second ≥ the record second (the sheet keeps whole seconds). A balance for an earlier day = end of that day. **Card opening owed** (Setup) holds every entry dated before its As-of date (`L.before`), so they are not counted twice. **Save message:** `impactToast()` after every save/delete shows each account/card old → new, or why it did not move (not linked, no balance yet, inside a recorded balance, before card tracking, your own bill figure); the Paid-with hint (`acctHint`) says the same before saving. **Setup → Accounts → Bank buttons on Add entry** (`payLinkHtml`, `linkVaultBank`) links each Vault bank to a Wealth account; choosing "Not in Wealth" sets `solo` so `syncPayMethods` never re-guesses. `syncPayMethods` links by same name, else the single Bank account whose name holds every word of the Vault label.
  - Tests (rebuild from this description): Code.gs in a vm with fake Sheets + Playwright Pixel 7, app internals exposed by injecting `window.__ev = s => eval(s)` before `start();` in the served copy only. End-to-end (20 checks through the real forms), a 250-step random add/edit/delete run against an independent reference (0 mismatches, same after reload and on a second phone), and syncPayMethods cases.
- **Cash in hand** = live balance of the Cash account(s), on Overview, in liquid money and net worth. `config.cashStart` is no longer used.
- **Wealth = tree:** "Debts" is now **Liabilities**. Assets and Liabilities group by kind: a kind with several accounts is one expandable row (`details.wtree`, open state kept in `state.openTree`), a single account stays a row; Credit cards open into each card (total owed, billed + pay-by date, next bill, later). The Liabilities breakdown (`w-debt`) has the same tree.
- **Card spends count on the purchase date** (owner confirmed); the card ledger shows which bill they land on.
- **Still to spend** (`budgetLeft`) = sum over budgeted, non-unplanned categories of max(0, budget − spent).
- **Credit cards = one object** (`config.cards`, linked to the Vault row id). Setup edits name, bill day, pay-by day (blank = about 20 days), limit, opening owed; removal goes to `config.cardsRemoved` (names kept for old entries). `syncPayMethods()` only ADDS new Vault cards; it never edits or re-adds cards.
- **Adjustments** (Setup): saved as ordinary entries tagged `Adjustment`. "Counts in the month" = expense in a chosen category (optional card; − uses the refund flag). "Card bill only" = Transfer category `cardadj` ("Card bill adjustment", `role: 'cardadj'`, created on first use) dated on the chosen statement date; changes card owed, not spending. `isCardPay()` excludes `cardadj`; entry buttons hide it.
- **Discretionary:** not shown in Setup at all (unplanned categories are hidden from the categories editor); still an expense category on the entry buttons, counted in the month. Last tab **Discretionary** (`renderDisc`): total at the top (paid − received), Paid / Received, All time or the selected month, rows Date · Description (wraps in full; tag pill + payment under it) · Amount (refunds in green "received"), grouped by month with paid/received subtotals; **tags**: your own labels in the entry's Tag, offered as one-tap buttons in the entry form when Discretionary is chosen (`discTagChips`, pref `discTags`), tag filter chips with totals in the tab; tap a row to edit; + Add opens the entry form on Discretionary.
- **Entries filters:** category, type, payment (Cash, each bank · bank, each card · card, Not recorded), search.
- **Breakdowns ("how it is worked out"):** every figure on Overview (KPIs, category tiles, Cash in hand, card-bill rows), Wealth (KPIs, section headings, each account, Credit cards, Still to spend, snapshots, FD cards) and Card bills (KPIs, each card header) has `data-act="why" data-k="…"` (via `whyAttr`). Tapping opens `#whyScrim`; `whyBuild(k)` builds sections of signed lines that add up to each total, from the same functions the screens use (`wealthCalc`, `cardLedger`, `cashInHand`, `budgetLeft`). Lines can drill down (`why-go`, with Back) or open an entry for editing. The sheet refreshes after each sync.
- **Keyboard rules (house rule, owner insists):** (1) no Android/Google autofill bar on any box except the sign-in email (`#mEmail`, marked `data-keep-ac`; the owner wants suggestions there). `noAutofill()` turns every `<input>` (except checkbox/radio/date/file/hidden) and every editable `<textarea>` into a contenteditable `.tbox.tin` (`toTextBox`); it runs automatically on every `innerHTML` write (Element.prototype.innerHTML is wrapped) plus a MutationObserver, so new boxes are covered without extra work. The boxes keep `.value`, `.type`, `.placeholder`, `.select()`, fire `input`, and fire `change` on leaving or Enter; Enter submits the form if it has a submit button. Select them by class/id, never by the `input` tag. (2) Never redraw a screen while someone types in it: `render()`, `setView()`, resize and the sign-in screen use `holdIfTyping()`; the skipped redraw runs when focus leaves the box.
- **Address above the keyboard:** that strip is Chrome's own address bar (owner has it at the bottom), not SwiftKey or the app; a web page cannot hide it. The fix is to run the installed app (manifest `display: standalone`): on phones in a Chrome tab, Overview shows a dismissible "Install the app" card (`install-go`, pref `instHide`), More has "Install as an app", Setup keeps its "Use it as an app" card; all use `beforeinstallprompt` or show the Chrome menu steps.
- **Keyboard awareness (Nov 2026, owner's #1 complaint: "top third frozen, keyboard covers what I type"):** viewport `interactive-widget=resizes-content`; `kbCheck()` (visualViewport/resize/focus events, phones only via `pointer: coarse`) sets `html.kb` when a typed box has focus and the visible height drops below 80% of the tallest seen for that orientation. In `.kb`: top bar slides away, bottom bar/FABs/save bar fade out, `main` loses its top padding, sheets go full height with no rounded corners, and `.sheet-actions` (sticky) sits right above the keyboard. **Every sheet (Nov 2026 second pass, "same mess in Discretionary / Edit entry"):**
  - **Every `.scrim` is sized to the visual viewport** (`top:var(--nvt); height:var(--nvh)`, set by `noteVV()` on visualViewport resize/scroll), not just the notes one. A plain `position:fixed; inset:0` keeps the pre-keyboard height on iPhone, putting fields and buttons behind the keyboard.
  - **`reveal(el)` / `keepInView()`** is the one mover. It scrolls only the element's own `.sheet` (or the page when there is no sheet), instantly and only by the pixels needed to sit inside the visible band: above the sheet's sticky `.sheet-actions`, or between the top bar and the nav bar. For a box taller than the band it keeps the caret (`caretRect`) in view instead. It waits while a sheet is still sliding in (transform ≠ none). Runs on focus, on keyboard resize, on visualViewport scroll (iPhone) and on every `input` (rAF). **Never go back to `scrollIntoView({block:'center', behavior:'smooth'})`**: it scrolls every ancestor and fights the browser's own focus scrolling. That was the "cursor jumps up and down".
  - **`keepKb()`**: pointerdown/mousedown on a `.sheet button` / `.sheet summary` while a box in that sheet has focus is `preventDefault`ed, after a `boxChanged()` on pointerdown so `change` still fires. Without it, the button took focus, `focusin` → `kbCheck()` dropped `html.kb` at once, the button bar jumped ~70px before the finger lifted, and the tap (Save, Save & add another, Cancel) was lost.
  - **Toasts never block:** `pointer-events:none`; while a sheet is open (`html:has(.scrim:not([hidden]))`) or `html.kb` is set, they sit at the top; any tap (after 600 ms) or typing (after 1.2 s) clears them (`toastAway`).
  - **Form errors live in the button bar:** `errToFoot()` moves each `.sheet .err` that precedes a `.sheet-actions` into it (static sheets at start-up, vault forms in `vForm`), so the error is always on screen next to Save. `clearSheetErr` hides it on any input, chip, seg, calc or checkbox tap. With the keyboard up (or an error showing), the bar is solid and buttons sit on one 42px row.
  - Edit entry no longer auto-focuses the amount (no keyboard forced on open); Add still does. Entry, balance and vault sheets open scrolled to the top. Discretionary "+ New tag" just opens More and focuses Tag (no toast).
  - Tested in headless Chromium at 390×844 → 390×400 (keyboard): Discretionary + Add, amount/note/tag fields visible above the bar, Save & add another works with the keyboard up, toast clears on tap, error shown in the bar and cleared on typing, Edit entry with no auto keyboard, Setup boxes, desktop sheet, notes editor unchanged.
- **No sideways scrolling anywhere (owner's #2 complaint):** every chip row wraps (`.chips` flex-wrap; Income/Transfer chips after a full-width `.chipsep` line); the tab strip is gone (navigation below); tables with class `rt` (card bill history, budget-cycle bridge) turn into stacked label/value rows via a **container query** whenever their card is under 720px (phones, or two card tiles side by side on a laptop) — each `<td>` carries `data-l` for its label; the Trend heat map becomes colour squares on phones (amounts in `.hv` hidden; average moves under the name as `.havg`; month header year in `.hy` hidden); the vault paste preview stacks too. Tested with `hscroll.js` at 360, 412 and 1440px.
- **Fixed bugs worth remembering:** never redraw screens with text boxes on `resize` (Android keyboard flapping); auto-focus only if focus isn't already in the form; vault screen redraws only when its state changes.

- **Card wording (owner's rule):** the statement date is always **"Stmt Date"** and the payment deadline **"Pay By"**, everywhere (Vault card face, Vault column, Setup, Card bills, Overview, breakdowns, entry hint). Never "Bill on/Bills on/bill of" or "pay by" for these dates. Vault card face shows Pay By from the card's Setup pay-by day (`vPayBy`).

- **Minus sign on spending (owner's rule):** on Add entry, a negative amount on an **Expense** (e.g. −300 on Miscellaneous) is saved as a refund of the same size, so it adds back to the balance, the category and the card. Not allowed on Income, Transfers or card bill payments (message says so). Bank balances may go negative; a negative bank shows under Liabilities in Wealth.

## Design system ("calm glass", Nov 2026 redesign; the owner asked for an Apple-class look and feel)
- **Shell:** one DOM for every screen size. `.shell` holds `#topbar` (logo, month picker `#cycBar`, sync dot `#status`, `#textBtn`, `#themeBtn`, `#addTop`) and `#navbar` (links with `data-v`; `.nv-2` = secondary screens; `#navMore`). **Phones/tablets (<1024px):** fixed glass top bar (58px + safe area) that slides away on scroll-down and back on scroll-up (`html.tb-hide`), a floating glass bottom bar with Overview, Entries, Wealth, Notes, More; the round `+` FAB above it (hidden on Notes, Discretionary, Setup, Vault); **More** (`openMore()`, `#moreScrim`) = tiles for Card bills, Trend, Discretionary, Setup, Vault (owner) + Day/Night, Text size, Install, Sync now. `Aa` lives in More on phones. **Laptop/desktop/TV (≥1024px):** `.shell` becomes a fixed glass sidebar (`.topbar/.tb-in/.tb-act` are `display:contents`, items ordered with `order`), page title `#pageTitle`, content max width `--vw` (1180px; 900px for Entries/Notes/Discretionary/Setup). **TV:** `--fsv` scales all type (1.12 at ≥1600px, 1.4 at ≥2400px) and focus rings thicken for remote D-pads. `markNav()` sets `aria-current`; the month label is `#cycLabel` with the real `<select id="cycPick">` laid invisibly over it (native picker, never clipped).
- **Motion:** screen changes use the View Transitions API (`navTo()`; content cross-fades, the bars do not), the new screen's cards rise in (`viewEntered()` → `.view.enter`, staggered, bars grow) and you start at the top; sheets slide up on a spring (`linear()` easing with a cubic-bezier fallback) over a blurred scrim (`@starting-style` + `transition-behavior: allow-discrete`, so `hidden` still opens/closes them); toasts rise in; buttons/chips scale on press. Everything collapses under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Android gets a 6ms tick on selection controls and 12–18ms on save/delete (`buzz()`).
- **Colours are roles** in `:root`, stepped separately for day and night, every text pairing checked to WCAG AA (≥4.5:1). Page `--paper` (#f2f3f7 day / #07080c night) with a soft fixed aura (`body::before`); cards solid `--sheet`; floating chrome uses `--glass` + `backdrop-filter`; inputs/chips/segments use translucent `--fill` tints; primary buttons/FABs use the blue `--btn-a → --btn-b` gradient; headline figures sit on the **hero gradient** (`--hero-a/b/c`, deep navy → blue with sky/teal glows) via `.card.kpis` (the KPI marked `.head` is the headline: Spent on Overview, with a bar against the budget and the even-pace marker; Net worth on Wealth; Billed to pay on Card bills) and `.thero`/`.why-big`/`.vhero`. Status: `--good` green = on track, `--warn` amber = running ahead of the month, `--bad` red = over budget, `--pur` purple = spent outside the plan (unplanned categories like Discretionary, no budget, Unlisted). Each has `-soft` and `-ink` steps; a status colour always sits beside a word. Accent is blue (`--accent`, kept away from purple). Don't reuse status colours for anything else. Card bill tiles use each bank's colours (`vTheme()`, same palette as the Vault).
- **Icons:** an inline SVG sprite at the top of `<body>` (`#i-*` for the interface, `#c-*` for categories), drawn with `<svg class="ic"><use href="#…"/></svg>`. `catIco(c)` picks a category icon from its ID and name (`CAT_ICON`, first match wins; order matters) and tints it (income green, transfer grey, unplanned purple). App icons in `icons/` are rendered from the same logo mark (gradient square + rising line).
- **Status logic** lives in `catStatus()` (one category) and `cycleOutlook()` (whole month: projected spend at the current daily rate; Fixed/Sinking-group bills count as paid in one go, never "running fast"). Overview tiles show a thin "even pace" marker.
- **Day/night**: `#themeBtn` flips `html[data-theme]`, remembered in localStorage (`budgetTracker.theme`); an inline script in `<head>` applies it before paint. Everything uses CSS variables, so charts switch without redrawing.
- **Type**: Inter for all reading and numbers (Archivo only for the name), body 17px; nothing below 13px; section headings are sentence case (no more all-caps labels). Big figures carry class `fit` and `fitNums()` shrinks them just enough to fit (never an ellipsis: `.fit` clips).
- **Text size and font** (`Aa` button, `textSheet()`): six fonts (Inter default, Atkinson Hyperlegible, Noto Sans, Roboto, Lexend, phone's font) and five sizes (0.9–1.35). Every font size in CSS and inline styles is `calc(Npx*var(--fs,1))`, so one variable scales the app; never write a bare px font size. `--fs` is a registered `@property` number = `--fsu` (your Text size, set by the head script and `applyText()`) × `--fsv` (screen scale for TVs). Saved in localStorage `budgetTracker.text`, applied in `<head>` before paint; `bigtext` class (size ≥ 1.1) puts the Entries filters in one column on phones. Chart label widths multiply by `fsNow()` (Text size × screen scale). Bottom-bar labels cap at 15px so they never truncate.
- **Overview card summary** (`cardStripHtml`): three figures (To pay now with urgency tone, Next bills, Total owed), an owed split bar, "Pay now" rows with calendar date tiles (red overdue, amber due within 5 days), "Coming up" rows, and a count of cards with nothing due. Every row opens that card's breakdown.
- **Notes tab** (4th tab, after Wealth; owner wanted it within reach on a 6.5" phone). A small floating "+ Note" button (bottom right) opens a **plain-text** note with the cursor in the text (no choices to make); Markdown/HTML/JSON are in the "Plain text ▾" pop-up at the bottom left of the editor; choosing one shows the note formatted at once (Markdown/HTML preview, JSON checked and tidied), with Edit/Preview for those kinds. Bottom bar (owner-specified, keep exactly): kind pop-up, then Copy, Delete (two taps, "Sure?") and ✓ Save (saves and returns to the list), side by side on one line; no ⋯, no Tidy button. Notes tab: no banner text. Top row (owner-specified, one line on any phone): **All ▾** (menu: All notes, Others, any added people, "+ Add a person"), **Arhaan**, **Efrah**, and a round search icon fixed at the right (grid layout). The icon opens a full-width search box with a 🎤 button that starts the phone's speech recognition (`SpeechRecognition`, en-IN; a page cannot press the keyboard's own mic) and ✕ to fold back. Added people are labels too: stored in pref `noteLabels` and on notes; the script accepts any safe name up to 24 characters (`okLabel`). Viewport uses interactive-widget=resizes-content so the bar sits above the keyboard. Labels: **Arhaan, Efrah, Others** (owner said "Arhan/Ifra"; we used the spellings already in the app; default from the signed-in email or last used). Pinned notes sit in a "Pinned" section; newest pin goes on top; order changes by drag grip (≡) or the press-and-hold menu (Pin/Unpin, Move up/down, Label, Delete with two taps). Untitled notes are named by their first line (list and Drive file name). Toolbar buttons keep the selection and keyboard (pointerdown preventDefault).
  - **The editor, rebuilt (Nov 2026, owner: "can't see what I type, cursor jumps, top third frozen, keyboard hides everything").** Root cause: the sheet was `height:100%` of a `position:fixed` scrim (so it kept the pre-keyboard height), and the writing area was **three nested scrollers** (`.sheet` → `.nbody` → `.ntext`) that each fought the browser's own scroll-the-caret-into-view, and the sheet's grid column sized to the toolbar's max-content width, pushing every row sideways. Now:
    - `#noteScrim` is sized to the **visual viewport** (`--nvh` height, `--nvt` top, set by `noteVV()` from `visualViewport` resize/scroll). That is the part of the screen the keyboard has not covered, on Android (where `interactive-widget=resizes-content` shrinks the layout viewport) and on iOS/WebViews (where it does not) alike.
    - `.sheet.note` is a `display:grid` with `grid-template-columns:minmax(0,1fr)` and rows `auto auto minmax(0,1fr) auto` → header / title+labels / **the one and only scroller `#nEdit`** / toolbar+buttons. `overflow:hidden` on the sheet; nothing else scrolls vertically.
    - `caretIntoView()` nudges `#nEdit.scrollTop` by the few pixels needed (never smooth, never the page), on input and on arrow/page keys; `keepInView()` skips anything inside `#noteBox` so the two never fight.
    - **Focus mode:** `#nText` focus adds `.writing`; on a phone or with the keyboard up (`html.kb` / `.tight`) the title, labels and the bottom button row fold away, and the pin becomes a **Done** button that drops the keyboard. The note's name always shows in the header (`.nttl`, "Untitled note" when blank); tapping it unfolds the title and labels (`.showmeta`).

## Access: entry-only links and the kill switch (Nov 2026)
- Roles: Owner, Member (Users tab, email or Google sign-in) and Entry. Entry has no sign-in: the owner makes a link in Setup → Entry-only links (`#entry=<64-hex token>`, stored as script property `elink:<token>`). Made for the owner's wife.
- The server is the lock. For an Entry user, Code.gs accepts only `txn`, `deltxn` (only for entries that link added, tracked in the EntryLog tab) and note ops. Its reply holds a trimmed config (categories without budgets, Bank/Cash accounts, cards without limits), everyone's entries (read-only) and `mine` (ids it added; only those can be changed). No balances, card figures, users, links, vault.
- The app shows Entry users three screens only: `add` (a big Add entry button and "What you added"), `entries` (everyone's, read-only; tapping someone else's entry says only its author can change it) and `notes`. `setView` sends every other hash to `add`; other screens are never drawn. Balance hints and impact toasts are off for Entry. A phone already signed in never swaps to an entry link.
- Switching a link off (Setup) deletes the property; that phone gets `signed_out` and drops back to the sign-in page.
- Kill switch: the owner sets a kill code in Setup (min 8 chars; stored as a salted 1,000-round SHA-256, `KILL_CODE`). `{kill: code}` or `{revive: code}` needs no sign-in (Setup, the closed screen, or the sign-in screen's "Owner: kill switch"). While `KILLED` is set, every request gets `app_killed`; each phone wipes cache, outbox, vault and notes copies (keeps its sign-in) and shows "This app is closed", then comes back by itself after a reopen. Five wrong codes lock it for an hour. Backup: run `killApp()` / `reviveApp()` in the script editor.
- Test: scratchpad `t/roles.js` (60 checks: server attacks plus owner and entry phones).
